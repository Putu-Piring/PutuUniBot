## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity

**Putu Piring Uni** is an authentic student-run culinary enterprise established by Diploma in Library Informatics students at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM) Kedah. The venture specializes in handcrafted, freshly steamed traditional *Putu Piring* infused with aromatic pure *Gula Merah* and served with freshly grated white coconut (*Kelapa Parut Putih*). 

The venture operates under a strict **100% Pra-pesanan (*Pre-order Only*)** operational model to ensure zero food waste, optimal ingredient freshness, and piping-hot delivery directly to customers.

---

## 2. Strategic Narrative & 4 Value Proposition Dimensions

Putu Piring Uni delivers value across four critical dimensions: **product performance, convenience, risk reduction, and affordability**:

1. **Product Performance & Peak Freshness:**  
   Unlike conventional cafeteria snacks that sit cold in open trays, our putu piring is delivered warm using **thermal polyfoam boxes**. By adopting a strict 100% pre-order system, every piece is steamed fresh moments before delivery rather than sitting in open display cases. Wrapping each portion in traditional **kertas minyak** and transporting them in thermal polyfoam boxes locks in heat, moisture, and fragrance, ensuring the putu piring reaches students in peak condition (**panas, lembut, dan gebu**).

2. **Risk Reduction (Coconut Spoilage Elimination):**  
   We address the primary failure point of traditional kuih—**rapid coconut spoilage**—by **salting, pre-steaming, and separately packaging the coconut**. This eliminates the risk of premature souring, ensuring customer satisfaction even if consumed later in the evening.

3. **Delivery Convenience & Zero Transport Friction:**  
   For just **RM 2.00 plus a RM 0.50 runner fee**, dorm and rental house students receive fresh heritage food without leaving their rooms. Internal student runners deliver directly to residential college lobbies, guard houses, and housing gates.

4. **Affordability:**  
   Priced at only **RM 2.00 per set (2 pieces)**, the product offers exceptional value for students balancing tight daily allowances.

## 4. Osterwalder's Channel Framework (5 Phases)

According to Osterwalder's channel framework in Chapter 4 of the ENT300 syllabus, Putu Piring Uni integrates physical and digital channels across all five channel phases:

1. **Awareness:**  
   Initial buzz is generated through targeted physical paper flyers and free taste-sampling sessions within student clusters, reinforced by peer-to-peer WhatsApp broadcast links and Status stories.
2. **Evaluation:**  
   Customers review clear digital menu flyers and pre-order delivery schedules posted across WhatsApp and Telegram networks.
3. **Purchase:**  
   Transactions are conducted directly via WhatsApp and Telegram messaging (@putu_uni_bot), accompanied by mandatory advance payment verification via DuitNow QR or Touch 'n Go (TnG) QR screenshots to prevent bogus orders.
4. **Delivery:**  
   The value proposition is physically fulfilled by internal team runners utilizing motorcycles and cars, meeting customers at agreed checkpoints (dorm lobbies, guard houses, and house gates) within designated evening slots using thermal polyfoam boxes.
5. **After-Sales:**  
   Messaging channels serve as an immediate follow-up channel to collect feedback, address any concerns, and encourage repeat orders.

## 5. Customer Relationships (Dedicated Personal Assistance Model)

Under Chapter 4 of the ENT300 syllabus, customer relationships focus on customer acquisition, retention, and loyalty. Putu Piring Uni employs a **Dedicated Personal Assistance model**:

1. **Rapport Building:**  
   We initiate contact on a personal level by asking customers for their preferred nickname, creating an informal yet respectful peer relationship.
2. **Transparency:**  
   Customers receive real-time updates as their food transitions from the steaming pot to the delivery runner, minimizing anxiety and missed handovers.
3. **Active Feedback Collection:**  
   After each delivery run, a standardized appreciation and feedback message is dispatched, reinforcing brand care and providing data for continuous culinary refinement.
4. **Service Recovery & Risk Mitigation:**  
   In the event of transit mishaps (e.g., squashed packaging from motorcycle travel), we operate a strict **"no-questions-asked" recovery protocol: instant fresh replacement or full refund via DuitNow**, managed directly by our dedicated PICs (Aiman and Nasrul). This builds lasting trust and word-of-mouth referrals across campus.

Mengikut Bab 4 buku teks ENT300, Putu Piring Uni menjana aliran masuk tunai melalui dua model pendapatan pelengkap:

## 8. Aktiviti Utama (*Key Activities — Chapter 4*)

Mengikut Bab 4 sukatan pelajaran ENT300, Aktiviti Utama merangkumi aliran kerja operasi kritikal bagi merealisasikan cadangan nilai:

1. **Disiplin Ambilan Pesanan & Waktu Tutup (*Order Intake & Cut-Off Discipline*):**  
   Bagi menjamin sifar inventori basi, operasi berjalan mengikut waktu cut-off yang ditetapkan dengan ketat. Pesanan diperuntukkan ID Pesanan unik secara automatik, dan bayaran pendahuluan (DuitNow/TnG QR) atau terma tunai (COD) disahkan sebelum proses penyediaan dapur bermula.
2. **Pengeluaran Berkualiti Terkawal (*Quality-Controlled Production*):**  
   Krew dapur mengayak tepung beras halus bersama daun pandan wangi, menyusun lapisan gula merah segar, dan mengukus atas permintaan (*steam on demand*) menggunakan acuan logam asli bagi memastikan tekstur yang lembut dan gebu.
3. **Pembungkusan & Pengoptimuman Laluan (*Packaging & Route Optimization*):**  
   Hidangan yang baru dikukus dibalut dengan kertas minyak tradisional untuk memerangkap haba, kemudian dimuatkan ke dalam beg penghantaran bermotosikal yang berpenebat haba / kotak polifoam. Penghantaran dikelompokkan mengikut zon geografi merentasi tiga zon utama (**Kolej Kediaman UiTM, kluster rumah sewa RPTU, dan Fasa 5 Lembah Bujang**).
4. **Penutupan Operasi Harian (*Daily Close-Out*):**  
   Pada akhir setiap syif, runner menyerahkan kutipan tunai fizikal kepada ketua pengurusan, semakan kepuasan pelanggan diselesaikan melalui Telegram, peralatan memasak disanitasi rapi, dan bahan mentah diinventori untuk kitaran operasi seterusnya.
